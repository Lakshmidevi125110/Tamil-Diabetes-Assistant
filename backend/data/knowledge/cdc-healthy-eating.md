# Healthy eating with diabetes (CDC)

## The main idea
The CDC page describes managing blood sugar as the key to living well with diabetes, and healthy eating as the key to managing blood sugar. The goal is to eat healthy foods, in suitable amounts, at suitable times, so that blood sugar stays within the target range as much as possible.

## What the page covers
The page itself is a short overview. It points readers to separate articles on:
- meal planning, including the plate method, carbohydrate counting and portion sizes
- choosing healthier carbohydrates
- fibre
- hidden sugars in everyday foods
- desserts and eating out

## Who can help
The page advises working with a registered dietitian or a diabetes educator to build a healthy eating plan. It does not name specific foods to include or avoid, so detailed food advice should come from that kind of professional or from other trusted sources in the knowledge base.

This is general educational information summarised from the CDC page. It is not an individual diet plan.
